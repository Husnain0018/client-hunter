How it works

  1. Enter the access code you were given and fill in the form.
  2. Press Find my leads. The system searches Google Maps, audits each business and writes your messages. This takes 3-6 minutes.
  3. Open any lead to see its score, problems found, competitors and a ready email and WhatsApp message.
  4. Copy the message, or open it in your email app / WhatsApp with one click.
  5. Optional: connect your own Google Sheet so every lead is also saved in your Drive.

Every search only returns new businesses - the ones you already received are skipped automatically.

Send email

Review the email, edit anything you like, then press Send. It goes out from the service mailbox and replies come to your email address.

Send WhatsApp message

The business name and number are saved to your Google Sheet first, then the message is sent from your own WhatsApp (through WAHA). The status shows Sent, and later Delivered or Read, once WhatsApp confirms it.
